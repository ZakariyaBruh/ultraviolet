About this demo

Real screens, nothing faked

  • Every picture is a screenshot of the real Friction app, taken in a real browser. The click zones were measured from the page itself, so they sit on the real buttons.
  • The history you see (the chart, the score, the rings) is sample data, and the app flags it as sample data. In the real app it is yours, and one tap erases it.
  • Radio, TV, podcasts, music, news, books, lessons, rooms and the leaderboard need the network, so they run only in the real app. The demo says so when you click them.
  • The demo plays one 25 minute session. The ruler shows the other lengths, then goes back to 25 when you lock in.
  • Retro and ASCII dials are shown in the Station look. Everything else works in all five looks.
