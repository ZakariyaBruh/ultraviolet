# Project guidelines

- Use EasyUI (easyui.site) components everywhere. All 131 are installed in `src/components/ui`; there is no demo view, so use them in the real app. Add more with `npx shadcn@latest add Surajmaurya1/easyui/<slug> -y`.
- Read `PRODUCT.md` (who and why) and `DESIGN.md` (the look) before any UI change. The look is a broadcast station: blue-black ground, one yellow, ice data, Hanken + Barlow Condensed + Unbounded, test-card bars, ASCII torus, contour field. Retro against futuristic. Avoid monospace body text, dot-matrix and pixel type and a single red-orange accent (that is another brand's look). No pixel-art or shard decoration: it was tried and removed. No glass, blur, gradients or glow in the app.
- Surfaces: `.glass` is the floating layer (dial, player, timer), `.material` is content. Flat, ruled, small radius. Full spectrum lives only in the colour-bar strip and the landing colour-bar strip. Themes live in `lib/themes.ts` plus `html[data-theme]` blocks in `index.css`; use tokens (`bg-ground`, `text-ink`, `bg-signal`, `text-on-signal`), never hex, in app code.
- No scroll guard. The site simply never scrolls; navigation is the tab deck (`NavDeck.tsx`) and routes (`lib/route.ts`). The measured unit is a drift: leaving the tab during a session.
- The app is a no-vertical-scroll, single-screen experience. Page lists (`useGridFit` + `SlidePagination`) or use tabs/slides instead of scrolling. `overflow-hidden` on `#canvas` is a safety net, not a layout strategy. Mobile hit targets are at least 44px.
- Themes differ in structure, not just colour (phosphor CRT frames, blueprint grid and dashed frames, sunset pills and sun, paper manga); Settings has accent and background pickers (`settings.accent`, `settings.bg`, applied in `lib/theme.ts`).
- Locked in, the speed warp runs at full strength behind the app and panels turn part-transparent (`html[data-locked]`) so the rush of stars shows through; no blur.
- Time counts only while locked in: `engine.ts` ticks only for a running session whose tab is visible and focused, and never credits a long gap.
- Search on Radio and TV looks at every station and every iptv-org category, not just the chip you are on. Screen changes zoom in a touch, a picked-up panel grows 3%, and a drift gives the screen a small zoom ripple.
- Every panel carries `data-movable` and is always draggable: grab it anywhere that is not a control, throw it, it leans and glides (`Arrange.tsx`, offsets in `lib/layout.ts`, localStorage `friction:layout`; Alt+arrows from the keyboard). Podcasts (`/podcasts`) use the iTunes Search API plus an RSS parser in `lib/podcasts.ts`, and the EasyUI morphing blob. Paper (`html.light`) is a manga page: ink keylines, hard offset shadows, screentone, grain, Dela Gothic One.
- News (`/news`) reads the feeds in `lib/feeds.ts` through `lib/news.ts`; hidden topics are `store.mutes` (word + expiry, always with undo). Google Analytics 4 (G-CZ1GG7FPZ1) is in `index.html`, page visits only.
- Celebrations: `useUI.celebrate` shows the EasyUI rocket-party-popper card (`Celebration.tsx`) on a finished session, a finished lesson and a sharp trivia round. Loading screens use the EasyUI morphing shape loader through `LoadingBlock.tsx`. Insights has a Rings tab of donut charts.
- Learn (`/learn`) builds lessons by rules from Wikipedia text (`lib/learn.ts`; the lesson itself uses no AI): idea cards, then scored steps in rotation (fill-the-gap choice, true or false, type the missing word, estimate on a slider, put events in order, match sections to their opening lines, build the sentence from pieces), a walkable timeline, and one interactive demo after the second idea. That demo is the built-in one for the topic when there is one (`demoFor`); a topic without one gets a demo written on the fly by Gemini (see the AI demos bullet). Steps are `Step` kinds; `SCORED` kinds must be answered before Continue, each reports through `onResult(ok)` and has a Show me skip; a miss leaves a card in the review deck (`cardFor` in `LearnView.tsx`). Years are hidden in ordering items until answered (`hideYear`) and BC dates are never offered as AD years. UI for the formats is `components/app/LessonSteps.tsx`; demos are in `components/app/demos/` (`kit.tsx` frame, sliders, chips; `science.tsx`, `mind.tsx`; `index.tsx` maps a lesson title to a demo with `demoFor`: orbit, cipher, natural selection, coin law, dilemma, limiting factors, scales, double slit, dichotomy of control, herd immunity, reaction time, perspective, Roman numerals, Egyptian doubling, compounding, climate sensitivity). Demos state their simplifications in a note, never invent data, animate only what the user starts (and jump to the end under `calm()`), and keep every control at 44px. While a lesson is open the page header is hidden to give the panel room (an iPad at 1000x750 has about 430px). Plus Open Trivia DB and a Leitner review deck in `store.learn`. Panels are independent (each has its own saved offset); the player and nav keep one position everywhere and, on screens where the user never put them over something on purpose, panels are eased aside (`tidy` in `Arrange.tsx`). Deliberate overlaps are remembered in `layout.keep`. Interactive SVG inside a panel carries `data-no-drag` or the panel moves instead.
- AI demos (`api/demo.ts`, `lib/aidemo.ts`, `components/app/demos/AiDemo.tsx`): when a lesson opens on a topic with no built-in demo (built-in always wins and makes no request), `useAiDemo` asks `GET /api/demo?t=<title>`; the function (env `GEMINI_API_KEY`, optional `GEMINI_MODEL` list as `model` or `model:thinkingLevel`, default `gemini-3.5-flash-lite,gemini-3.1-flash-lite,gemini-3-flash-preview:low,gemini-3.5-flash:minimal,gemini-3.7-flash:low,gemini-3.8-flash:low`; 2.5 Flash is closed to new keys; a 429 rests a model for 30 minutes and a 503 for one; a 400 retries once without the thinking setting; the next model is started after `HEDGE_MS` (9 s) without waiting for the first to fail and the first good answer wins, within a 50 s budget; free keys allow only about 20 requests a day per model, so enable billing for steady use) pulls the Wikipedia summary for grounding, asks Gemini for TITLE/TASK/NOTE/HTML sections, and refuses code that names the network, storage, eval, the parent window, external URLs or endless loops (`parse`). Good answers are cached for a month at the edge (`s-maxage`) and in IndexedDB (`learn:demo:v1:`), so each topic is written once; limits are 10 requests an hour per IP, same-origin only (`sec-fetch-site`). Only the topic title leaves the browser; the key never does and must never be in git or the client. The demo is untrusted code: it runs in `<iframe sandbox="allow-scripts">` (no `allow-same-origin`, so an empty origin) with a CSP meta that allows only inline script and style, so nothing can read the page, its storage, or send anything out; the page reads only the words ready, touch and error from it. It is labelled AI-made and says to check it against the article. The page calls `prefetchDemo` the moment a topic is tapped so the answer is usually back by the time the demo step comes. The demo step is a slot that is always in the lesson when `settings.aiDemos` is on (default; Settings switches it off): pending shows a loader with Continue enabled, ready shows the frame, failed (no key, error, timeout after 70 s, bad answer, a script that errors before it loads) skips the slot (`skip`, `nextAt`, `prevAt` in `LessonPlayer`) so the lesson never fails because of it. A demo that breaks after loading shows a Start it again bar. The frame gets the app's colours as `--ink --ink2 --ink3 --ground --signal --on-signal --rule` and base styles from `frameDocument`; on wide screens the text sits beside the frame so it gets the height. Test with a scratch script that calls the handler with the key in the environment, then through the lesson with `/api/demo` intercepted, at 1440x900, 1000x750 and 390x844 in both themes.
- Together (`/together`, `api/room.ts`): rooms of up to 12 by a six-character code; browsers poll (serverless cannot hold sockets) on a budget set by `delay()` in `lib/together.ts`: 3 s on the Together screen while something is changing, 6 s when it is quiet, 8 s on other screens, 12 s with the tab hidden; each poll is one round trip to Turso (`api/room.ts`, fast path in `sync`) because the free Vercel plan allows 1M function calls a month. Radio, TV and podcast episodes can be shared (an episode carries its position); a friend gets a Tune in toast because browsers may block sound they were not asked for. `vercel.json` gives `/assets/` immutable caching so returning visitors cost one request. Chat, a shared lock-in, a shared radio/TV item, and an optional Discord or Slack incoming webhook that the host sets (stored server-side only, validated to those hosts). Two names exist on purpose: the private profile name (onboarding, friends only) and the public leaderboard alias (asked on the Leaders screen). Notifications go through `lib/notify.ts` (the bell, plus system notifications when the tab is hidden); the board is watched in `lib/watch.ts`.
- All user data lives in the browser (`src/lib/store.ts` for localStorage, `src/lib/cache.ts` for IndexedDB). The only server code is `api/lb.ts` (the recommendation leaderboard) and `api/room.ts` (Together rooms), both on Turso (env `TURSO_URL`, `TURSO_TOKEN` on Vercel, never in git or the client). The score needed for the board is 0 while there is room (about 4.5 GB), and the lowest score plus one once the database is full: a newcomer who beats it replaces the lowest person. Aliases on the board are unique (folded to lowercase letters and digits, so "Zak", "zak" and "Z.a.k" are one name; a few words like "You" are reserved), and people without an alias show as "Friend XXXX". Do not add other backends. The one other function is `api/demo.ts` (lesson demos, below), which keeps no data.
- Motion is a feature but it must answer something: respect the `motion` setting (0 calm / 1 lively / 2 lots) and `prefers-reduced-motion`. User-triggered motion settles under 300ms; exits use ease-in.
- Honest data only: never invent proof; demo history is flagged (`demo: true`) and removable. No chat anywhere, and AI only writes lesson demos (never answers, tutoring or the Debrief, which is plain computed sentences).
- Before shipping UI: `npx tsc -b`, `npm run build`, an axe pass, and screenshots at 1440x900 and 390x844.
- Era dial: `settings.era` (100 future to 0 past, default 80, six stops of 20) sets `--retro` (stage/5) and `html[data-retro]` 0-5 in `lib/theme.ts` (`eraStage`); Settings shows the six stops as buttons plus a snapping slider. The further back, the more 3D text solids and the more of the page is text, not the more screen filter: screen effects (scanline, grain, warmth, heading glow) read `--fx`, which stops at Broadcast (`min(stage, 2)/5`), so nothing past stop 2 adds a filter or a curve. Stops: 0 Future (clean; one text solid in the timer and beside page titles), 1 Afterglow (scanline, grain, warmth; one torus turning in the corner), 2 Broadcast (OSD, pixel kickers, two solids), 3 Arcade (Silkscreen titles, hard offset shadows, three solids and a perspective floor of text lines, three solids in headers), 4 Terminal (dashed panel outlines, a `>` prompt before page titles, text stars, five solids), 5 Full ASCII (seven solids and a whole text world behind everything, square panels, heavier dashes). Body text stays in the normal face at every stop; pixel type is only for titles, kickers and HUD lines. The engine is `lib/ascii3d.ts` (point-cloud shapes torus, sphere, cube, knot, helix, pyramid; z-buffer, half-Lambert light, `RAMP`); `AsciiScene.tsx` is the canvas behind the app (per-stage scene table `SCENES`, strength `GAIN`; it measures panels and every line of text on screen every 0.6 s and draws only 55% behind panels and 22% behind text so reading never fights it); `AsciiSolid.tsx` is the small `<pre>` renderer (timer, page headers via `HeadSolids` in `shared.tsx`, landing, shop; `interactive` lets a drag turn it). Never add a tube, bezel, rolling bar, ticker, sprite, chromatic split or all-text pixel face: those were the clunky version and were removed (`RetroBits.tsx` and `Sprite.tsx` are gone). The pixel-art ban above still applies at stops 0-1. `PhoneBreak.tsx` is the onboarding illustration (a phone in flames in front of a wheel, cable snapped, notifications flying off); export in `public/break-the-connection.png`.
- Credits, the Arcade and the Shop (`/arcade`, `src/games`, `lib/credits.ts`): `store.credits` (bal, earned, spent, best per game, log, items) is earned only by real work. Focus pays by a clean streak: 1 credit a minute, 1.5 after 10 clean minutes, 2 after 20, 3 after 35, 4 after 60 (`TIERS`/`rateAt`; a drift resets the streak, a bought Break does not; `session.credit` accrues in `tick`, the Focus dial shows the live rate and the next tier). A finished session pays +5 and +5 more if you never drifted (`finishBonus`). A finished lesson pays `lessonCredits` (3, +1 per right answer, +4 for a clean sheet), once a day per lesson; trivia pays `triviaCredits` (1 per right answer past four, +3 for a perfect round) for three rounds a day. A round costs `COST` (4) credits, or the game's own `cost` (Descent 6), and is blocked while locked in. 16 canvas games share `games/engine.ts` (480x320, keys + pointer + on-screen pad, palette read from theme tokens, 75 s cap or the game's own `round`); add one by exporting a `Game` and listing it in `games/index.ts`. Losing with time left offers one restart per round for `CONTINUE_COST` (2) credits that keeps score and time (`g.revive()`; a game opts out with `noContinue`, as the roguelite does). The Shop tab (`components/app/Shop.tsx`, one page per item in `components/app/shop/`; catalogue `SHOP` in `lib/credits.ts`, counts and unlocks in `credits.items`, bought through `buyItem`) sells mostly things you use: Break (25: five minutes off in the middle of a lock-in, the timer waits, leaving the tab is not a drift, recorded on the session) and Long break (45, twelve minutes), Streak shield (40: the next drift keeps your clean streak, still counted as a drift, shown as `session.shielded`), 50:50 lifelines (20 for five: remove two wrong answers in a lesson check or trivia), Time capsule (20: a note to yourself shown in the celebration after your next finished session, `sealCapsule`), Sound machine (120: rain, wind, fire, waves and hum synthesised from filtered noise in `lib/ambience.ts`, a mixer in a Sheet from the Focus screen, remembered in `items.mix`), Break kit (60: breathing guide and stretch deck on the timer during a break, `BreakKit.tsx`), Solids pack (90: pick the solid that turns in the timer), Fidget spinner (30: pointer-flick physics, a tick sound, a turn counter kept forever), Confetti cannon (12: the same card as a finished session) and the Golden Brick (5,000, does nothing, can be pressed for a thud and a counter). There is no Pause: a Break is the only way to stop the timer mid-session, it costs credits and is limited to what you have bought. Items that act on a session (breaks, shield, kit, sound machine) are used from the Focus screen; the spinner and the cannon are put away while locked in. No gambling mechanics: credits are never won in games and prices are fixed. The engine gives every game a 3-2-1 countdown, pause (P, Esc, tab hidden), particles, shake and synthesised sound (volume setting; shake and particles follow the `motion` setting and `prefers-reduced-motion`; a mute toggle is remembered in `friction:arcade-mute`). Every game must also be playable from the keyboard and, on touch, from the on-screen pad (`Game.pad`). The engine also has hit-stop (`g.freeze`), flashes, banners, shockwaves, a score multiplier chip (`g.mult`), a seeded procedural music loop that follows `g.intensity`, and `g.light/hi/lo` so shadows and highlights suit light themes. Games were pruned to the ones that earned their place (Catch, Flap, Hurdle, Aim and Whack were removed); Rally, Breakout, Snake, Invaders, Asteroids, Stack, Crossing, Orbit and Descent (a roguelite: rooms, three upgrade cards, a boss every third room) are the polished tier: new games should match that bar (layered background, telegraphed hazards, power-ups, combos, banners, hit-stop, music intensity). Smoke-test new games by bundling `games/index.ts` with esbuild (`--define:import.meta.env.DEV=false`) and stepping each `make()` with a mock `G`.
- Long passages never run into controls: wrap body text that can vary in length in `FitText` (`components/app/shared.tsx`), which shrinks the font until it fits its box (used for Learn idea cards and checks). Anything with a variable length that sits beside other things (ordering rows, match rows, a review card, a trivia question) does the same. Check lessons and demos by measuring: no element outside the panel, no `scrollHeight > clientHeight`, no overlapping siblings, at 1440x900, 1000x750 and 390x844 in the station and Paper themes.
- The end-session slider (`.slide-end` in `index.css`) is restyled to the station's voice (flat, ruled, signal yellow, condensed capitals); keep it, and keep new confirm sliders to the same look.
