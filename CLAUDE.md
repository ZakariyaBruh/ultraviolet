# Project guidelines

- Use EasyUI (easyui.site) components everywhere. All 131 are installed in `src/components/ui`; there is no demo view, so use them in the real app. Add more with `npx shadcn@latest add Surajmaurya1/easyui/<slug> -y`.
- Read `PRODUCT.md` (who and why) and `DESIGN.md` (the look) before any UI change. The look is a broadcast station: blue-black ground, one yellow, ice data, Hanken + Barlow Condensed + Unbounded, test-card bars, ASCII torus, contour field. Retro against futuristic. Avoid monospace body text, dot-matrix and pixel type and a single red-orange accent (that is another brand's look). No pixel-art or shard decoration: it was tried and removed. No glass, blur, gradients or glow in the app.
- Surfaces: `.glass` is the floating layer (dial, player, timer), `.material` is content. Flat, ruled, small radius. Full spectrum lives only in the colour-bar strip and the landing colour-bar strip. Themes live in `lib/themes.ts` plus `html[data-theme]` blocks in `index.css`; use tokens (`bg-ground`, `text-ink`, `bg-signal`, `text-on-signal`), never hex, in app code.
- No scroll guard. The site simply never scrolls; navigation is the tab deck (`NavDeck.tsx`) and routes (`lib/route.ts`). The measured unit is a drift: leaving the tab during a session.
- The app is a no-vertical-scroll, single-screen experience. Page lists (`useGridFit` + `SlidePagination`) or use tabs/slides instead of scrolling. `overflow-hidden` on `#canvas` is a safety net, not a layout strategy. Mobile hit targets are at least 44px.
- Themes differ in structure, not just colour (phosphor CRT frames, blueprint grid and dashed frames, sunset pills and sun, paper manga); Settings has accent and background pickers (`settings.accent`, `settings.bg`, applied in `lib/theme.ts`).
- Locked in, the speed warp runs at full strength behind the app and panels turn part-transparent (`html[data-locked]`) so the rush of stars shows through; no blur.
- Time counts only while locked in: `engine.ts` ticks only for a running session whose tab is visible and focused, and never credits a long gap.
- Search on Radio and TV looks at every station and every iptv-org category, not just the chip you are on. Screen changes zoom in a touch, a picked-up panel grows 3%, and a drift gives the screen a small zoom ripple.
- Every panel carries `data-movable` and is always draggable: grab it anywhere that is not a control, throw it, it leans and glides (`Arrange.tsx`, offsets in `lib/layout.ts`, localStorage `friction:layout`; Alt+arrows from the keyboard). Podcasts (`/podcasts`) use the iTunes Search API plus an RSS parser in `lib/podcasts.ts`, and the EasyUI morphing blob. Paper (`html.light`) is a manga page: ink keylines, hard offset shadows, screentone, grain, Dela Gothic One.
- News (`/news`) reads the feeds in `lib/feeds.ts` through `lib/news.ts`; hidden topics are `store.mutes` (word + expiry, always with undo). Google Analytics 4 (G-CZ1GG7FPZ1) is in `index.html`, page visits only.
- Celebrations: `useUI.celebrate` shows the EasyUI rocket-party-popper card (`Celebration.tsx`) on a finished session, a finished lesson and a sharp trivia round. Loading screens use the EasyUI morphing shape loader through `LoadingBlock.tsx`. Insights has a Rings tab of donut charts.
- Learn (`/learn`) builds lessons by rules from Wikipedia text (`lib/learn.ts`: idea cards, fill-the-gap and which-came-first checks, no AI), plus Open Trivia DB and a Leitner review deck in `store.learn`. Panels are independent (each has its own saved offset); the player and nav keep one position everywhere and, on screens where the user never put them over something on purpose, panels are eased aside (`tidy` in `Arrange.tsx`). Deliberate overlaps are remembered in `layout.keep`.
- Together (`/together`, `api/room.ts`): rooms of up to 12 by a six-character code; browsers poll (serverless cannot hold sockets) on a budget set by `delay()` in `lib/together.ts`: 3 s on the Together screen while something is changing, 6 s when it is quiet, 8 s on other screens, 12 s with the tab hidden; each poll is one round trip to Turso (`api/room.ts`, fast path in `sync`) because the free Vercel plan allows 1M function calls a month. Radio, TV and podcast episodes can be shared (an episode carries its position); a friend gets a Tune in toast because browsers may block sound they were not asked for. `vercel.json` gives `/assets/` immutable caching so returning visitors cost one request. Chat, a shared lock-in, a shared radio/TV item, and an optional Discord or Slack incoming webhook that the host sets (stored server-side only, validated to those hosts). Two names exist on purpose: the private profile name (onboarding, friends only) and the public leaderboard alias (asked on the Leaders screen). Notifications go through `lib/notify.ts` (the bell, plus system notifications when the tab is hidden); the board is watched in `lib/watch.ts`.
- All user data lives in the browser (`src/lib/store.ts` for localStorage, `src/lib/cache.ts` for IndexedDB). The only server code is `api/lb.ts` (the recommendation leaderboard) and `api/room.ts` (Together rooms), both on Turso (env `TURSO_URL`, `TURSO_TOKEN` on Vercel, never in git or the client). The score needed for the board is 0 while there is room (about 4.5 GB), and the lowest score plus one once the database is full: a newcomer who beats it replaces the lowest person. Aliases on the board are unique (folded to lowercase letters and digits, so "Zak", "zak" and "Z.a.k" are one name; a few words like "You" are reserved), and people without an alias show as "Friend XXXX". Do not add other backends.
- Motion is a feature but it must answer something: respect the `motion` setting (0 calm / 1 lively / 2 lots) and `prefers-reduced-motion`. User-triggered motion settles under 300ms; exits use ease-in.
- Honest data only: never invent proof; demo history is flagged (`demo: true`) and removable. No AI and no chat anywhere: the Debrief is plain computed sentences.
- Before shipping UI: `npx tsc -b`, `npm run build`, an axe pass, and screenshots at 1440x900 and 390x844.
- Era dial: `settings.era` (100 future to 0 past, default 80, six stops of 20) sets `--retro` and `html[data-retro]` 0-5 in `lib/theme.ts`; Settings shows the six stops as buttons plus a snapping slider. Stops: 0 Future, 1 Afterglow (scanline, grain, warmth), 2 Broadcast (OSD, pixel kickers), 3 Arcade (Silkscreen titles, stepped corners, hard shadows, pixel pointer, marquee ticker in `RetroBits.tsx`, landing sprites), 4 Tube TV (all text pixel type, curved glass, rolling bar, split-colour titles), 5 Full 8-bit (Press Start titles, dithered bordered panels, bezel, sprites in the ticker). Pixel type and sprites appear only through this dial, never at the default (the pixel-art ban above applies at stops 0-1). `PhoneBreak.tsx` is the onboarding illustration (a phone in flames in front of a wheel, cable snapped, notifications flying off); export in `public/break-the-connection.png`.
- Credits and the Arcade (`/arcade`, `src/games`): `store.credits` (bal, earned, spent, best per game, log) is earned only by real work: 1 per focused minute and +5 for a finished session (in `endSession`), +6 for a finished lesson (once a day per lesson), +4 for a sharp trivia round. A round costs `COST` (4) credits and is blocked while locked in. 15 canvas games share `games/engine.ts` (480x320, keys + pointer + on-screen pad, palette read from theme tokens, 75 s cap); add one by exporting a `Game` and listing it in `games/index.ts`. No gambling mechanics: credits are never won in games. The engine gives every game a 3-2-1 countdown, pause (P, Esc, tab hidden), particles, shake and synthesised sound (volume setting; shake and particles follow the `motion` setting and `prefers-reduced-motion`; a mute toggle is remembered in `friction:arcade-mute`). Every game must also be playable from the keyboard and, on touch, from the on-screen pad (`Game.pad`). The engine also has hit-stop (`g.freeze`), flashes, banners, shockwaves, a score multiplier chip (`g.mult`), a seeded procedural music loop that follows `g.intensity`, and `g.light/hi/lo` so shadows and highlights suit light themes. Games were pruned to the ones that earned their place (Catch, Flap, Hurdle, Aim and Whack were removed); Rally, Breakout, Snake, Invaders, Asteroids, Stack, Crossing and Orbit are the polished tier: new games should match that bar (layered background, telegraphed hazards, power-ups, combos, banners, hit-stop, music intensity). Smoke-test new games by bundling `games/index.ts` with esbuild and stepping each `make()` with a mock `G`.
- Long passages never run into controls: wrap body text that can vary in length in `FitText` (`components/app/shared.tsx`), which shrinks the font until it fits its box (used for Learn idea cards and checks).
- The end-session slider (`.slide-end` in `index.css`) is restyled to the station's voice (flat, ruled, signal yellow, condensed capitals); keep it, and keep new confirm sliders to the same look.
